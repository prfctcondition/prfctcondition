<h3 id="manifesto">manifesto · philosophy</h3>

independent engineer building native windows software with zero bloat. i believe convenience, seamless tools, and music are fundamental human rights — as essential as air and food. software shouldn't gatekeep comfort or rent-seek on sound; it should serve freely and quietly. otofy was born directly from this conviction.

i leverage modern ai as an unapologetic force multiplier. architectural taste, systems intuition, and low-level engineering remain strictly human — ai collapses iteration cycles, enabling solo execution at 10x velocity.

> 「 music and convenience are basic human rights · craft with intent, ship without bloat 」

<h3 id="ecosystem">the zen ecosystem · unified desktop precision</h3>

an interconnected ecosystem of focused, zero-bloat windows software engineered for competitive gamers, creators, and power users alike. whether isolating cpu cores to eliminate micro-stutter in fast-paced games, piercing pitch-black night raids via hardware display lut curves in tarkov and dayz, or streaming soundscapes in high-fidelity—the zen suite unifies essential tools into one lightweight, telemetry-free experience.

> 「 clarity in darkness, precision in silicon, stillness in focus 」
